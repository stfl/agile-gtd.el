#+title: Changelog

* 0.3.0

** Breaking change: ~agile-gtd-max-priority-group~ is removed

Upgrading from a release that carried ~agile-gtd-max-priority-group~: the
defcustom and the global priority override behind it are removed, and setting
it has no effect. A single global cutoff cannot express "this view is
deliberately narrow, that view is deliberately wide", which is the whole point
of per-buffer range rotation. Its interactive role — widening the sprint
threshold on the spot — is served by ~agile-gtd-agenda-wider-range~.

~agile-gtd-sprint-prio-threshold~ is untouched and sets the =sprint= cutoff;
~agile-gtd-backlog-priority-threshold~ is likewise untouched and governs only
the =rS= SOMEDAY review command.
